Discovery Shield

A quick-commerce return that is easy but verified — not "no questions asked". Opt in before you order, get a tamper seal, return in two taps and one photo.

  1. Add items, turn on Shield in the cart
  2. Order → sealed at dark store → delivered
  3. Open the order → Return within 2 hours
  4. Reason → one photo → AI verdict in seconds
  5. Agent collects → seal scan → refund

Use the ⚙ button on the phone to force a failed photo check, a broken seal, or an exhausted quarterly quota.
