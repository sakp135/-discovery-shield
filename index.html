🔍 Step 1 — AI Life Signal Detection

AI reads your order history to detect life context signals — new baby, new pet, fitness journey, gifting occasion, late-night impulse window.

🛒 Step 2 — Contextual Nudge at Cart

At cart screen — when your wallet is already open — ONE relevant new category suggestion appears. Not three. Not a list. One, with a personalized reason why it fits you right now.

🛡️ Step 3 — Discovery Shield Activates

Your first purchase in any new category is automatically protected. Easy return within 24 hours, no questions asked. This removes the biggest barrier our research identified — fear of being stuck with a bad product.

🔄 Step 4 — Habit Reinforcement

After successful trial, a follow-up nudge encourages the second purchase. This converts a one-time trial into a new category habit — solving the 75% drop-off problem our interviews revealed.

📊 Research Behind This

Primary research (N=7 interviews, July 2026) showed:

• 66.7% would try new category IF return guaranteed
• 83% discovered new categories only through urgency
• 75% tried once then stopped — habit never formed
• Trust gap: Grocery 4.3/5 vs Non-grocery 3.2/5

Discovery Shield directly addresses all four findings.
